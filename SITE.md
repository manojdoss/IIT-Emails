## Pages

### 1. Home
Hero-driven landing page that establishes IIT as a center of excellence in integrative medicine and clinical research. Highlights key services, active clinical trials, and a clear call-to-action to book a consultation.

### 2. About
Mission, vision, and story of IIT — Minnesota's first psychedelic medicine clinic. Communicates the multidisciplinary team approach and commitment to evidence-based, cutting-edge therapies.

### 3. Staff / Our Team
Profiles of clinical leadership and practitioners, starting with founder Dr. Manoj Doss, DO (board-certified physician, addiction medicine specialist). Each profile includes credentials, specialties, and a photo.

### 4. Healing (Services Overview)
Parent page that introduces the full spectrum of treatment modalities offered at IIT. Links out to each individual service page below.

#### 4a. Ketamine Infusions
IV ketamine infusion therapy for treatment-resistant depression, anxiety, PTSD, and chronic pain. Covers protocol details, expected outcomes, and safety information.

#### 4b. Ketamine Assisted Psychotherapy (KAP)
Combines sub-anesthetic ketamine with psychotherapy sessions. Explains the integrated model, session structure, and candidacy criteria.

#### 4c. SPRAVATO (Esketamine)
FDA-approved nasal-spray esketamine for treatment-resistant depression. Details eligibility, in-office administration requirements, and insurance coverage.

#### 4d. IV Therapy
Vitamin, mineral, and nutrient infusions for wellness optimization, recovery, and immune support. Lists available formulations and their benefits.

#### 4e. Longevity Medicine
Lifestyle and preventative therapy services focused on long-term health optimization, metabolic health, and age-related wellness strategies.

### 5. Research
Overview of IIT's role as a clinical research site. Describes the mission to expand access to novel treatments through participation in rigorous clinical trials.

#### 5a. COMP360 Psilocybin in TRD
Details on the COMPASS Pathways COMP360 psilocybin trial for treatment-resistant depression — enrollment status, eligibility, and what participants can expect.

### 6. Partnerships
Information for organizations, providers, and sponsors interested in collaborating with IIT on clinical care, research, or referral pathways.

### 7. Pricing
Transparent pricing for all services. Covers self-pay rates, package options, insurance/SPRAVATO coverage details, and financing information.

### 8. Contact
Contact form, phone number, address, office hours (Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM), and embedded map. Sets expectation of response within 1 business day.
